THE GAME

Welcome to a massive, ever-growing universe where every vehicle matters and every job counts. Build your own transportation network across a living world filled with cities, oceans, mountains, industries, rail yards, airports, docks, and highways.

Drive realistic trains, operate cars and trucks, pilot planes, and command boats and cargo ships as you move passengers, freight, mail, fuel, and valuable goods. Earn money, buy new vehicles, unlock routes, expand your fleet, and master each transportation mode.

Everything is simulated: smooth track switching with physics-based train movement, road deliveries and logistics jobs with passengers, long-range flights and cargo runs, heavy freight shipping and coastal missions.

Upgrade, customize, automate routes, run AI fleets, and become the world's greatest transport tycoon. Move everything. Build your empire.

03/

UNDER THE HOOD

Every locomotive in this game is manufactured, not downloaded. Engineering blueprints are drafted, 3D-modeled and render-checked by agents driving Blender through MCP, then built into Roblox places our agents edit and playtest directly.

  • Blueprint-first design: chassis, running gear, guidance and constraints defined before any block is placed
  • King class and Prairie 2-6-2 locomotives modeled in Blender via MCP — parts exported as registered GLBs with ID and QC sheet
  • Running system engineered for real steam geometry: coupled drivers, proper rod phasing, bogie capture physics
  • Agents operate Roblox Studio directly through MCP: build, script, playtest, instrument, iterate
  • Derailment and alignment verified with measured playtests — offsets in studs, logged
