05/OUTTAKES — THE BLOOPERS WE KEPT

TAKE 01 // TRANSMISSION UNKNOWN
Full alien broadcast
We asked for a demo reel intro. The model invented a language instead. Linguists welcome.
TAKE 02 // IT CAN READ
The mascot reads the instructions out loud
It was told to preserve the audio rules. It started reciting them. In fluent gibberish.
TAKE 03 // STILL READING
Prompt-reading incident, part two
"Persecuting audio's original words and sequence." Sir, that is an instruction, not a line.
TAKE 04 // FARMING INCIDENT
"The real cockpit never leaves the land"
We said LAN. The voice heard land. Now the cockpit is apparently a wheat field.
TAKE 05 // WHO?
Byrus DP-DT runs fully offline
Never heard of him. Sounds like a different, slower mascot. He is not on the payroll.
TAKE 06 // ECHO CHAMBER
"...a tycoon in the forge. In the forge."
Said it twice. The second one was for emphasis it did not ask for.
TAKE 07 // BROKEN SPRINKLER
Road, air, water, water
The roadmap has four modes of travel now. Two of them are water.
TAKE 08 // FORGOT THE NAME
"Negzis is our agent mesh"
All-caps brand words break the voice. We now spell them like humans. Negzis remains unclaimed.
TAKE 09 // WRONG GUY'S CORDS
The day the mascot showed up with someone else's voice
Words perfect, pitch wrong. We now measure the voice itself, not just the words.
TAKE 10 // TOO HONEST
"That is how we would eat this elephant"
Cut for being too honest about how we actually work. The elephant line lives here now.
TAKE 11 // CONTINUITY CRIME
He ran so far the universe changed behind him
Perfect run, perfect wave, new physics at the horizon. The best take of the bunch.
